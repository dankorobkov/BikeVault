02 — Iconography

Isometric chunky set

3D · cartoon · theme-aware

Each icon is a stack of geometric solids drawn in 30° isometric perspective. Three-tone faces (light top, mid-right, dark left) keep them solid and readable. The active palette accent drops in as the highlight stripe so they retint as you swap palettes.
