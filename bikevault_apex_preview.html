Rules

Do

  • Keep ½-stripe-width clearspace on all sides
  • Use master at ≥ 60 px, small below
  • Pair with Bone or Graphite for surrounding UI
  • Render the stripe in solid Signal red only

Don't

  • Recolor the stripe
  • Rotate, skew, or mirror the mark
  • Add gradients, glows, or shadows
  • Place on a busy or photographic background

Dynamic behavior

Three suggested motion moments where the mark earns its keep.

App launch

Stripe wipes in left-to-right (~700 ms). Slot pops in last (~80 ms) as the "vault closes" beat.

Pull-to-refresh

Stripe rotates 360° around the icon centroid while data loads. Slot widens slightly on completion.

Workout active

Slot pulses 4–6 px at the rider's cadence. The icon's only moving element becomes a heart-rate read.
